Does your business have a website?
What will MotiveStack look at?
  • Your homepage and a few more public pages of the same site (such as About, Services, Products, Locations and Contact) and the pages those link to, up to 8 pages in all.
  • Only the readable text. No scripts are run, no files are downloaded, and nothing is typed or submitted on your site.
  • Your site's robots.txt rules are followed. If your site blocks automated visitors or asks for a login, we stop and you can describe your business yourself instead.
  • The text is sent to the AI model (when AI is switched on) to suggest findings. In mock mode no AI is used.
  • Nothing you approve is saved to your Business Brain until you press Save.
