2. Information we collect

  • Connected advertising account data, when you authorize us through Meta: ad account identifiers and names, campaign, ad set and ad settings, performance metrics, and the Facebook Page you connect. We request only the permissions needed for the features you use.

  • Usage and technical data, such as log data, device and browser type, and actions taken in the product, used to operate and secure the service.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the service: read your advertising accounts, show results, prepare campaigns for your approval, and sync leads to the tools you choose.
  • To create or change a campaign only after you explicitly approve it.
  • To secure, maintain, and improve the service, and to communicate with you about it.
  • To comply with law and with the terms of the platforms we connect to.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use data received from Meta for advertising to you, to build profiles for unrelated purposes, or for anything other than providing the features you requested.
